Thank you for the review. We retested the build under review on a clean device and could not reproduce a crash, a dead end, an empty screen or a non-functioning feature.

Three behaviours are deliberate and can look unfinished if you move quickly.

1. A clean install leaves Home and Cookbook empty until you save a dish and open it. Home shows "Tonight's ticket" with "The pass is waiting." and "Open cookbook". Cookbook shows "The cookbook is empty." and "Search the catalog". Search with a blank field stays on "Name a dish." Type a "Dish name", tap "Save", "Close", then "Open".

2. After the last "Walk", Home becomes "The pass is clear." The plate reads "Service is marked." with "The dish is plated. Service is marked. Sleep is restored." That clear pass is intended, not a crash. Tap "Open another recipe" to continue.

3. "Walk" stays dim while bowls are still open. Use "File bowl" until mise closes. An early tap can show "File every mise bowl before the first walk." Then "Walk" files each fire line.

Full review path. No account, no login:
- Cold launch is a full-bleed splash with no text or buttons. The system notification dialog may appear; there is no custom pre-prompt.
- First launch shows four dark pages with page dots: "Tonight's ticket" (Next), "Walk the fire" (Next), "Plate the dish" (Next), and "Mise, then fire" (Continue). "Skip" is on every page. "Skip" or "Continue" lands on Home.
- Home has no tab bar. Bottom chrome: "Search the catalog", "Cookbook", "Fire rule", "Open settings", and the plated counter.
- From an empty pass tap "Open cookbook". From an empty Cookbook tap "Search the catalog".
- On Search, type a "Dish name". Tap "Save" on a row, then "Close". A quiet catalog can show "The catalog did not answer. These dishes are already on the shelf."
- In Cookbook tap "Open" to write tonight's ticket as "Mise" and return to Home. If a ticket is already open, rows say "Busy" and the note is "Tonight's ticket is still firing. Plate it before opening another recipe."
- Tap "File bowl" until "Every bowl is filed.", then "Walk" each fire line. "Retract" peels the last mark when allowed. While busy they stay dim ("Filing the bowl", "Filing the walk", "Retracting").
- "Fire rule" or the "Mise then fire" chip opens that sheet. "Close" or "Back to the ticket" returns to Home.
- Settings: "Pass notes", "Marks on this pass", "Replay the pass notes", "TheMealDB catalog", "Contact the pass" with "https://salamander-pass.pro/contact-us", and "Reset all data" ("Reset the pass" / "Cancel"). Replay or reset shows onboarding again.

On a device install the Cookbook starts empty until you "Save". Search can still list the local shelf: "Salt crust sea bass", "Charred lemon chicken", "Butter mushrooms", "Pass tomato salad", "Iron pork chop", "Herb oil beans". An open "Mise" or "Firing" ticket returns on relaunch until it plates or you reset.
